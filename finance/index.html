03

How my system works

Every trade idea goes through the same five gates. Platforms: TradingView, MetaTrader 4/5, Investing.com, Bloomberg Terminal at university.

01 / SIGNAL

Coded conditions

Entries written as explicit Pine Script conditions, never a gut read of a chart.

02 / FILTER

Confluence

RSI, MACD and moving averages must agree before a signal counts.

03 / RISK

Size and stop

Position size and stop set before entry, while the loss is still cheap.

04 / TEST

Backtest

Confirmed bars only, no lookahead, judged against buy and hold.

05 / REVIEW

Process over P&L

A win from a broken rule is still a broken rule. Logged in a trade journal.

Risk rules I trade by

1–2%Maximum capital at risk on any single trade
2:1 · 3:1Minimum reward to risk before I take a setup
StopsSet before entry and respected
DrawdownLimits treated as a first order constraint, not an afterthought
JournalStructured trade journals and Excel logs, reviewed for rule adherence

Macro · US

A hold is not a neutral signal

The Fed held at 3.75–4%, with two dissents in favour of a hike. The dissent matters as much as the decision.

Watch the votes, not only the rate
Risk

Drawdowns are asymmetric

A 50% loss needs a 100% gain to recover. That is why sizing and stops come first. Try it below.

−50% → +100% to break even
Backtesting

Repainting quietly lies

An indicator that changes its past signals flatters a backtest. I use confirmed bars and turn lookahead off.

barstate.isconfirmed · lookahead off in request.security
Evaluation

Beat buy and hold, not cash

A strategy that makes money but trails simply holding the index has not added anything.

Benchmark = the passive alternative
Behaviour

Discipline is the bottleneck

The thesis behind Tradenova: rules beat discretion, because the hard part is doing what you planned.

Judge rule adherence, not outcome

+100%Gain needed to recover

Required gain = 1 ÷ (1 − loss) − 1. The curve steepens fast, which is why I cap risk per trade at 1–2%.
